About me...

Hi, I’m Sebas! I’m a Data Scientist, graduated from Henry, with expertise in Python and SQL from my previous studies at UTN. I also really enjoy Data Analysis with Power BI and making intelligent data-driven decisions, integrating it with SQL.

I am currently Co-Founder & CTO (Chief Technology Officer) of Bico, a company based in Colombia that specializes in data analysis and technology solutions, primarily for the logistics sector. At Bico, we build intelligent systems that create efficient and optimized routes using Machine Learning, helping businesses reduce costs, improve delivery times and make smarter operational decisions. I lead the entire technology stack — from algorithm design and ML model development to the web platform, internal tools and geospatial mapping modules.

I’m also working as a Python freelance developer on a cryptocurrency trading bot, developed in Python consuming the Binance API, automating strategies with PySide6 as the graphical interface, and integrating a full system for logging operations, charts and performance metrics. I’m currently developing Aether Bot 2.0, a new algorithmic crypto trading bot on Binance, which is in its final testing and backtesting phase. Once all legal coverage is in place, I plan to launch 5 SaaS licenses to the market.


I am deeply committed to continuous learning and staying ahead of the latest tools and methodologies in data science and software engineering. I believe in the power of multidisciplinary teams, where diverse perspectives transform complex data findings into actionable, real-world impact — something I experience every day building Bico from the ground up.
